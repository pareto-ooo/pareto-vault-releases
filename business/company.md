## Judging a project proposal

Use these questions during discovery, next to the architecture principles:

1. **Fit.** Which goal or objective above does it serve, and which value does it express?
2. **Customer.** Does it bring Pareto closer to its customers, or does it put distance between customers and specialists?
3. **Improvement.** What does it improve, measurably: customer satisfaction, retention, growth, cost, risk or speed to market?
4. **Scale.** Does it work for growth without adding exceptions or manual work, or is it a one-off?
5. **Ownership.** Is there a clear owner who answers for the result, and was the "why" settled before the "what"?
6. **Alignment.** Does it follow the enterprise principles, architectures, and goals?
